# Personal Knowledge Graph

Agent-Native Research Artifact for the local-first personal knowledge graph and
its rebuildable Anytype projections.

## Layers

- `logic/`: crystallized claims, problems, and implementation heuristics
- `src/`: executable research artifacts and environment records
- `trace/`: decisions, experiments, sessions, and process reasoning
- `evidence/`: raw evidence indexes and generated results
- `staging/`: observations awaiting an explicit closure signal
